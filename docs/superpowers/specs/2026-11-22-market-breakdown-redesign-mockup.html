What happened out there
The three stories everyone was squealing about.
1
Outlook went dark
  • Microsoft 365 had a global outage starting Nov 12.
  • Outlook, Teams, and Copilot were hit, with about 138,000 outage reports at the peak.
  • Microsoft says it found no sign of an attack.
🐷 Millions couldn't open their inbox. Personally, I've never opened one. No thumbs.
2
Planes stayed on the ground
  • On Nov 13 the FAA halted departures nationwide for about four hours.
  • The cause was a telecommunications failure.
  • Roughly 12,400 flights were canceled. Every plane in the air landed safely.
🐷 For once it wasn't the pigs who couldn't fly.
3
A cloud over Nvidia
  • On Nov 8, Nvidia's CEO told employees he had been unfaithful to his wife.
  • News reports say US intelligence believes a customer executive he was close to worked for Chinese intelligence.
  • That claim is unproven, and China denies it. Nvidia says it is reviewing the matter.
