Market Assumptions

FRED tests your plan using three market scenarios, each simulated 1,000 times.

Scenario Avg return Inflation
Conservative (8%) 8% / 20% vol 2.5%
Moderate (10%) 10% / 18% vol 2.5%
Optimistic (12%) 12% / 16% vol 2.5%

Each of the 1,000 scenarios draws annual returns from normal distributions (Box-Muller) calibrated to S&P 500 data, 1926-2024. Simulations model the FRED portfolio: 100% stocks (VTI · VXUS · VBR). Your withdrawal rises 2.5%/yr with inflation.
